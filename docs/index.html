Install

Three steps.
Under a minute.

  1. 1

    Download the APK. Android may warn you, because it comes from outside the Play Store.

  2. 2

    Open the file and allow installs from your browser, then tap Install.

  3. 3

    Open Kbrd, tap Enable keyboard, turn it on, then choose Kbrd. Allow the microphone for voice typing, and add an AI key under Set up AI if you want the assistant.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

What is Kbrd?

Kbrd is a minimalist Android keyboard with a full emoji library, a clipboard, a number pad, voice typing in English and Hindi, adjustable size and haptics, and an optional AI assistant.

Is Kbrd free?

Yes. Kbrd is free to download as an APK from the official GitHub releases page.

How does the AI assistant work?

Kbrd Free works out of the box with an open-source model hosted by OVHcloud, with no key. You can also paste your own API key from a provider such as Groq, OpenRouter or Google Gemini. When you tap an AI action, Kbrd sends only the selected text or the text before your cursor to the service and shows you the result before replacing anything.

Which AI models can I use?

The built-in Kbrd Free option uses an open-source model and allows about 2 requests a minute. With your own key you can use any model from an OpenAI-compatible provider, including free tiers and open-source models on Groq, OpenRouter and Together.

Is my typing private?

Kbrd has no servers and no analytics. It goes online only when you tap an AI action, never runs in password fields, and stores your API key encrypted with the Android Keystore.

How do I install the APK?

Download Kbrd-debug.apk, allow installs from your browser when Android asks, open the file, then open Kbrd and follow the two setup steps to enable and switch to the keyboard.

Does Kbrd support Hindi voice typing?

Yes. Choose हिन्दी in the voice panel, allow the microphone and speak. Saying “send” or “bhej” sends your message. Voice recognition uses your phone’s own speech service.
