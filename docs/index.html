Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don’t.

01

Minimal by design

Flat matte keys, thin icons and true AMOLED black. Pick from six accent colors, or switch to a light theme.

02

Instant response

Keys react the moment you touch them. Hold Backspace and it keeps deleting until you let go.

03

Emoji for everyone

Packs for kids, teens, adults and seniors. Scroll through every category or search by name.

04

Clipboard

Text you copy is saved automatically, so you can paste it again later with one tap.

05

Number pad and cursor

A proper numpad, a comma where you expect it, and a spacebar you can slide to move the cursor.

06

Made to fit

Resize the keyboard height and key length, and shift it left or right for one hand. Changes reach the keyboard instantly.

07

Voice typing

Speak in English or Hindi. Say “send”, “enter” or “bhej” and your message goes. Uses your phone’s speech service.

08

Haptics you control

Set your own vibration strength for every key press, from off to firm.

09

AI in WhatsApp

Smart help for writing and replying, right from the keyboard.

Install

Three steps.
Under a minute.

  1. 1

    Download the APK. Android may warn you, because it comes from outside the Play Store.

  2. 2

    Open the file and allow installs from your browser, then tap Install.

  3. 3

    Open Keymold, tap Enable keyboard, turn it on, then choose Keymold. Allow the microphone if you want voice typing.
