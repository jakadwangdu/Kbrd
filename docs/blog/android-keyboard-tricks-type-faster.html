10 Android keyboard tricks to type faster

Small habits make typing on a phone much faster. Here are ten that work on most Android keyboards, with notes on how Kbrd handles each one.

  1. Slide the spacebar to move the cursor. Drag your finger left or right along the spacebar instead of tapping the screen. In Kbrd a quick tap still types a space.
  2. Hold Backspace. Keep your finger down to delete continuously, then release when you reach the spot you want.
  3. Use a number pad for numbers. Switching to a proper numeric layout beats hunting through the top row. Kbrd has a dedicated number pad.
  4. Dictate long messages. Voice typing is often faster than thumbs. Kbrd supports English and Hindi and sends the message when you say “send” or “bhej”.
  5. Search emoji by name. Type “love” or “cricket” instead of scrolling through every category.
  6. Use the clipboard. Copy something once and paste it later from your saved history.
  7. Resize the keyboard. A taller or narrower keyboard that fits your hands reduces mistakes more than any other change. Kbrd lets you set key height, key length and position.
  8. Tune the haptics. A gentle vibration helps you feel each key without looking. Set a strength you like.
  9. Let AI fix and reshape text. Write quickly, then tap a rewrite or fix grammar action. See how to set up AI with a free key.
  10. Pick one layout and stay with it. Muscle memory builds fast when the keyboard stays the same.
