Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-25

Summary

Kbrd has no accounts, no analytics and no servers. It does not collect your typing.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (theme, key size, haptic strength), your clipboard history of copied text, and your AI provider choice are stored in the app’s private storage on your phone.

AI features and your API key

AI is optional. You paste your own API key from a provider you choose. The key is encrypted with the Android Keystore and is never sent anywhere except to that provider to authorize your requests. When you tap an AI action, Kbrd sends the selected text, or the text before your cursor, and for “Reply to copied” your most recent copied text, directly from your phone to your provider. Nothing is sent otherwise. AI actions are switched off in password and private fields. The provider’s own privacy policy applies to what it receives. You can remove your key at any time in the app.

Internet access

Kbrd asks for internet permission only to make the AI requests you start. Typing, emoji, clipboard and the number pad work without it.

Voice typing

Voice typing uses the speech recognition service on your phone, which may be provided by Google or another vendor. Kbrd receives the recognized text. The microphone permission is used only while voice typing is active, and the speech service’s own privacy policy applies to the audio.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Open an issue at github.com/jakadwangdu/Kbrd.
